# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Introduction
My favorite television series is **Stranger Things**. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers.

## About the Series
The story takes place in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of friends who discover supernatural mysteries and a dangerous alternate dimension called the Upside Down.

## My Favorite Characters

- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers
- Max Mayfield

## Why I Like This Series
I like Stranger Things because it combines friendship, adventure, mystery, and suspense. The characters work together to overcome challenges and protect their town.
